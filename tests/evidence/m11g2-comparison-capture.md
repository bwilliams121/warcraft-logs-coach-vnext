## Certification and coverage: NO grant

Owner decision (2026-10-11): a server-filtered capture earns a category grant
only from an exact certificate against a trusted WCL table over the same
interval and predicate. A failed, unavailable or mismatched certificate earns
nothing. `src/wcl/comparison/comparison-tables.ts` computes the certificates
from the committed bytes:

- **Matches.** Every owned pet's sourced damage matches its own DamageDone table
  exactly, per ability, in all three fights. So does the focus's own in fight 1.
- **Disagreements.** Every one is an event the TABLE omits. None is a table
  count the stream lacks:
  - fight 2 focus: 95 events of 1287955;
  - fight 3 focus: 10 events of 404908 and 3 of 410265;
  - every focus's damage taken: Spirit Link 98021;
  - pets' DamageTaken tables are empty.
- **No certifying table.** Casts tables omit melee, some triggered spells and
  every pet cast, and nothing certifies casts TARGETING an actor. No table
  exists for auras, resources, summons or combatantinfo.

No category certified, so every slice withholds its grants and records why
(`actorSlice.grantsWithheld`). The loader refuses any grant on a filtered
capture (`FILTERED_CAPTURE_GRANTED`). The composed fights hold every category
ABSENT. G2's facts read them as UNCERTIFIED_FILTERED_CAPTURE: counts are
AT_LEAST, raw sums UNKNOWN and collections OBSERVED_ONLY.

## Sanitization

Run `node scripts/corpus/sanitize-g2-comparisons.mjs .probe/m11g2 .probe/m11g1 <out>`.

- **Identities.** There is one pseudonym scheme, `g1PseudonymTables` from the G1
  sanitizer. Report codes become the G1 `COHORT###` codes. Every Player actor
  becomes its G1 `Player####`, and the sanitizer refuses one G1 never saw.
  Every other actor becomes `Actor<id>`.
- **Redactions.** Servers are null, player gameIDs are null, and rate-limit data
  is dropped.
- **Tables** keep ids, counts, totals, uptimes, bands and the window tables'
  bounds. They drop names, icons, source/target lists and hit/miss detail. The
  raid entry keeps per-target-TYPE totals, as G1 did for the subject.
- **Refusal.** The sanitizer refuses a credential shape, or any real name,
  server, report code or player gameID it saw.
- **Determinism.** Two runs are byte-identical.
