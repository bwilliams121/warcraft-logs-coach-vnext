  (345,177 ms).

  was resurrected, so the denominator is the whole fight, with neither downtime

## Sanitization

`node scripts/corpus/sanitize-g1-cohort.mjs .probe/m11g1 .probe/m11e2/context.json <out>`:

- **Pseudonyms.** Third-party names become `Player<nnnn>`, keyed by name alone,
  because the surfaces spell realms differently ("Tarren Mill" vs
  "TarrenMill"). Third-party report codes become `COHORT<nnn>AAAAAAA`. Both are
  numbered in SHA-256 order, consistently across all seven files.
- **Subject report.** It keeps its code, and its players become `Actor<id>`,
  matched by the M8-F rule.
- **Redacted and dropped.** Every server is null. Guilds, character ids, GUIDs,
  icons, item and enchant names, parse ranks and percentiles, rate-limit data
  and NPC actors are dropped.
- **Refusal.** The sanitizer refuses to write a credential shape, or any real
  name, server or third-party code it saw in the input.
- **Determinism.** Two runs are byte-identical.
- **Replay.** A discovery replay over the sanitized files reproduces the live
  replay's selection, rung, tally and member facts exactly.

The live report may show the members' real names, servers and log references;
the committed evidence never does.
