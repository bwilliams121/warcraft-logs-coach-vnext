# M13-A Augmentation Evoker evidence — provenance

Human-readable companion to the three `wcl-m13a-*` archive artifacts. The
machine record is `tests/evidence/manifest.json` (generated by
`scripts/corpus/build-manifest.mjs --root evidence`); this file explains it.
Nothing here is read by a test.

## Authority

Owner grant of 2026-11-01, `agent/autopilot-queue.md` "M13-A": one public
raid fight containing an Augmentation Evoker, chosen as the first eligible
candidate in a fixed neutral order (at most 10 candidate checks); for that
fight only context, roster, casts, the Ebon Might / Prescience / Breath of
Eons auras, deaths and identity; no damage, healing, resource, ranking or
comparison data; at most 50 Warcraft Logs requests.

## Selection (fixed before any request)

Plan: the gitignored `.probe/m13a/capture-plan.md`, written before the first
request.

1. One listing request: `reportData.reports(zoneID: 53, limit: 10, page: 1)`
   with each report's encounter fights (`id`, `encounterID`, `difficulty`,
   `kill`, bounds, `friendlyPlayers`, `friendlySpecs`). Zone 53 is The
   Venomous Abyss (Midnight, `frozen: false`), read from the committed
   b1PmaKrd6CDfzngc context. No ranking or performance field.
2. Order: report start time descending, then code; fights by id. Tiers:
   Mythic kill, Heroic kill, Mythic wipe, Heroic wipe, others. The first
   eligible fight (an encounter with "Augmentation" in `friendlySpecs`) of the
   best non-empty tier; subject the lowest such actor id.
3. Result: 46 eligible fights in the ten reports, none a Mythic or Heroic
   kill, so tier 3: a Mythic wipe of encounter 3429 (The Coiled Altar),
   fight 1, subject actor 1. The production fight-context gate confirmed it
   on the first attempt: accessible, fight and actor listed once, Player /
   Evoker, Augmentation at its `friendlySpecs` index.

Nothing about performance, parse or recipients was read before selection.

## Coverage: none granted

Every stream is server-filtered. Under the owner rule of 2026-10-11 a
filtered capture earns a grant only by an exact certificate against a
trusted WCL table; none was requested. The source records the capture-query
kind `FILTERED_EVENT_STREAMS`, and the loader refuses any grant on it
(`FILTERED_CAPTURE_GRANTED`). The composed bundle has CASTS and AURAS ABSENT
and DEATHS UNVERIFIED, so every fact read from it is OBSERVED_ONLY.
